Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Make sure your screens are set up and paired with OptiSigns.
2. Log in to the OptiSigns portal and navigate to **Files/Assets** then select **Apps**.
3. Click on the **YouTube** app.
4. Enter the YouTube video details:
   - **Name**: Name your video (the app may fetch the title automatically, but you can edit it).
   - **URL**: Paste the actual YouTube video URL (not the share link). For YouTube Shorts, modify the URL format from `/shorts/` to `watch?v=`, for example:
     ```
     https://youtube.com/shorts/BUXczOFByAU?si=TO8RGfmokQMdsI6R 
     ```
     becomes
     ```
     https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BUXczOFByAU
     ```
   - **Caption**: Toggle closed caption display if available on the video.
5. Click **Save** and optionally Preview the video.
6. Assign the created YouTube asset to your screen via the Screens tab or add it to a Playlist or Schedule.

This method is for single YouTube videos or Shorts. For playlists, channels, or live streams, there are other dedicated apps.
